FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions parents usually ask before booking a first session. Don't see yours? Send us a note.

How do sessions work?

Each session is one-on-one with the same tutor every week, so nobody has to re-explain where they left off. Sessions typically run an hour, in person or over video — whichever you and your tutor arrange.

What subjects and grade levels do you cover?

Math from pre-algebra through AP Calculus AB and BC, plus biology, chemistry, and physics — including AP Biology and AP Physics 1 — for students in grades 6 through 12.

Who are the tutors?

Firm Foundation has three tutors, each focused on their own subject rather than a single team teaching everything. See background and teaching approach for each on the Tutors page.

How much does it cost?

Each tutor sets their own hourly rate. Current rates by subject are listed on the Services & rates page.

How do we pay?

Payment is by Venmo, sent directly to your tutor — there's a "Pay via Venmo" link on each tutor's profile. Rates don't change based on format.

How do we book a session?

Pick your tutor on the Booking page and choose an open time on their calendar. You'll get a confirmation by email.

What if we need to cancel or reschedule?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice when possible — just reach out to your tutor directly. See the full policy on the Services page.

Is video tutoring just as effective as in person?

Both formats work well for one-on-one tutoring — it mostly comes down to what's more convenient for your family. If you choose video, your tutor sends a link before your first session.

Where do in-person sessions happen?

Location is arranged directly between you and your tutor.

Do we need to commit to a set number of sessions?

No. Sessions are booked individually through your tutor's calendar, so frequency is entirely up to you.

Still have questions?

Tell us the class and where your student is stuck, and we'll point you to the right person.
