FAQ

Don't see your question? Send us a note.

How do sessions work?

One-on-one with the same tutor each week. Sessions run about an hour, in person or online.

What subjects and grade levels do you cover?

Math through AP Calculus AB and BC, plus biology, chemistry, and physics (including AP Biology and AP Physics 1), for grades 6–12.

Who are the tutors?

Three tutors, each focused on their own subject. See backgrounds on the Tutors page.

How much does it cost?

Each tutor sets their own hourly rate. See the Rates page.

How do we pay?

By Venmo, directly to your tutor. Find the link on their profile.

How do we book a session?

Send a request on the Booking page with the days and times that work. Your tutor confirms by email or text.

What if we need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give 24 hours' notice when possible, directly to your tutor.

Is online as good as in person?

Both work well. Choose whichever is easier for your family. Your tutor sends a video link before the first session.

Where do in-person sessions happen?

Location is arranged directly between you and your tutor.

Do we need to commit to a set number of sessions?

No. Sessions are booked one at a time, so how often is up to you.
